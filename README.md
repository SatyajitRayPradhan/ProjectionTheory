# The Projection Theory (PT)

A deterministic, information-geometric framework that models the universe as a bandwidth-limited, lossy rendering rather than an isolated physical creation. 

---

## 1. Core Axiom: Lossy Geometric Projection
[cite_start]Our 4D spacetime universe is a non-injective, dimensional projection of an $N$-dimensional higher source ($N > 4$)[cite: 3, 227, 812]. [cite_start]Because mapping from a higher dimension to a lower one is fundamentally lossy, the absolute, objective nature of the higher source cannot be reverse-engineered or calculated with 100% certainty from within the 4D frame[cite: 32, 36, 228]. 

* [cite_start]**The Information Gap:** The mapping is many-to-one; multiple higher-dimensional configurations can yield identical 4D shadows[cite: 36, 37, 238].
* [cite_start]**The Epistemological Ceiling:** We are bound by the geometry of our dimensional container, making transcendental truth mathematically unreachable ($P = 0$)[cite: 38, 56, 95].

## 2. Sub-Theory 1: The Entropy-Symmetry Inversion & Inertial Cycle
[cite_start]This framework replaces the traditional Big Bang singularity with a smooth, geometric phase transition model of time[cite: 146, 274].

* [cite_start]**Symmetry as Maximum Entropy:** At the absolute limit of spatial expansion, all particles achieve perfect distribution and uniform spatial distance[cite: 116, 117, 124]. [cite_start]Randomness drops to zero, flipping maximum entropy into a state of absolute symmetry (Zero Entropy)[cite: 113, 118, 133].
* [cite_start]**The Inertial Over-Shoot:** Perfect symmetry is a temporary state, not a static freeze[cite: 289, 295]. [cite_start]Matter carries inherent momentum and inertia across this boundary[cite: 288, 296]. [cite_start]The moment particles pass this equilibrium point by a single Planck length, the symmetry breaks, restarting the arrow of time and the entropy cycle[cite: 297, 304, 305, 306].
* [cite_start]**The Horizon Problem Resolved:** Time resets globally because unique states and metadata (causal history, headers, parity bits) are bleached at the point of perfect symmetry[cite: 141, 310, 312]. [cite_start]The 96+ billion light-year physical extent of matter persists across infinite sessions, while our 13.6 billion-year timeline is simply the "uptime" of the current session[cite: 161, 316].

## 3. Sub-Theory 2: The Photonic Precursor & Sampling Bottleneck
[cite_start]Light is not merely an entity propagating *within* spacetime; it is the fundamental carrier wave *projecting* spacetime from the higher dimension[cite: 178, 179, 180].

* [cite_start]**Wave-Particle Duality:** This duality is the signature of un-collapsed projection data[cite: 785]. [cite_start]Before light interacts with the 4D screen, it exists in a state of high-dimensional superposition[cite: 572]. [cite_start]Upon settlement, it manifests as either localized Energy (Wave-state) or localized mass/matter (Particle-state)[cite: 573, 835, 836].
* [cite_start]**Matter Defined:** Matter is defined strictly as **energy occupying space**—localized latency where the projection data is confined to specific spatial coordinates in the 4D frame[cite: 169, 187, 188].
* [cite_start]**$c$ as a Hardware Refresh Rate:** The speed of light ($c$) is not an ontological physical speed limit, but the maximum operational sampling rate of our 4D spacetime buffer[cite: 206, 208, 832]. [cite_start]Objects or information moving beyond $c$ outrun the rendering capability of the local frame, becoming "unaccounted variables" omitted from our observable frame[cite: 210, 349].
